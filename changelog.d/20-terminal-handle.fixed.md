Live sender verbs pass the coordinator terminal handle as `--from` (check uses `--terminal`). The handle is `ORCA_TERMINAL_HANDLE` or adapter `from`. When live is on and no handle is available, those verbs throw `no-sender` and do not invent one. Live spawn also passes `--json` and copies ids only from the 1.4.240 result (`result.run.id` and the matching path per verb). Prose such as `Run run_… created and bound: …` is unparseable. A parsed `check` records `converged` as `false`. Dry-run and `awaiting_human_pr` are unchanged.
